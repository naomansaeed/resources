Using This Tool Offline (Print Version)

This printed version includes a manual calculation worksheet so you can complete the diagnostic without the interactive tool.

  1. Step 1: Fill in your baseline numbers in the inputs below (or use the estimation guidance if you don't have real data).
  2. Step 2: Go through each of the 8 diagnostic questions and check the box if you have that problem.
  3. Step 3: Use the calculation formula below to compute your monthly revenue leak.
  4. Step 4: Identify your biggest leak categories and prioritize fixes.
Manual Calculation Formula:
Baseline Monthly Revenue = Monthly Visitors × Conversion Rate × Avg. Project Value
Total Penalty % = Sum of penalties for each "Yes" answer
Lost Revenue = Baseline Monthly Revenue × (Total Penalty % / 100)

Example: 300 visitors × 3% conversion × $1,000 project = $9,000 baseline
If you answer "Yes" to Speed (20%) + Clarity (25%) + Mobile (15%) = 60% total penalty
Lost Revenue = $9,000 × 0.60 = $5,400/month leak

Phase 2: Identify Your Leaks

Check the box for each problem you have. Each "Yes" applies a specific conversion penalty based on industry research.

~20% penalty
How to check: Paste your URL into Google PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev). Is your Mobile performance score below 80?
Note: Google research shows 53% of mobile users leave a site that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Speed issues are often caused by unoptimized images or heavy themes — we can audit and compress your assets to fix this quickly.
~25% penalty
How to check: Open your homepage on a phone. Read only the largest text (the H1 heading). Does it state exactly what you do and who you serve in under 10 words, without scrolling?
Note: Nielsen Norman Group research shows unclear value propositions are the #1 reason visitors bounce. If your headline is vague, we can help you craft a clear, compelling message that instantly hooks your ideal client.
~15% penalty
How to check: Open your site on your actual phone. Is your primary "Book Now" or "Contact" button visible without scrolling, and can you tap it easily with your thumb?
Note: Baymard Institute research shows mobile interface friction accounts for ~15% of lost conversions. This is a common template oversight — we can redesign your mobile navigation and CTA placement for effortless tapping.
~10% penalty
How to check: Look at the top half of your homepage (before scrolling). Do you have at least 2 verifiable client testimonials, case study snippets, or recognizable client logos displayed prominently?
Note: Edelman Trust Barometer research shows lack of social proof causes ~10% of hesitant buyers to abandon. Just starting out? We can design a section that gracefully gathers and displays your first few testimonials as you earn them.
~15% penalty
How to check: Try filling out your own contact form. Does it require more than Name, Email, and a brief Message? Does it take more than 60 seconds?
Note: Baymard Institute research shows ~35% of form abandonments are due to overly long forms. Every extra field drops conversions. We can build streamlined, multi-step forms that integrate directly with your CRM to capture leads with zero friction.
~10% penalty
How to check: Look at your homepage. Is there exactly ONE dominant, visually distinct call-to-action button telling the user what to do next? Or are there multiple buttons of similar prominence?
Note: Hick's Law research shows decision paralysis from too many options causes ~10% of visitors to leave without acting. We can restructure your layout to guide visitors toward a single, clear action.
~5% penalty
How to check: Click every link in your header and footer. Check the very bottom of the page for the current year. Scan for any "Lorem Ipsum" or placeholder text.
Note: Stanford Web Credibility Research shows small neglect signals cause ~5% of visitors to doubt your professionalism. These details are easy to fix — a quick maintenance sweep can restore immediate credibility.
~5% penalty
How to check: Look at your browser URL bar for the padlock icon (HTTPS). Scroll to your footer — is there a clearly visible, clickable link to your Privacy Policy?
Note: GlobalSign survey research shows ~5% of cautious visitors leave sites without visible security indicators. We can ensure your site is fully compliant and secure, building instant trust with cautious visitors.
