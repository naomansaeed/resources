The Homepage Credibility Checklist

15 Essential, Objective Tests Every Service Business Homepage Must Pass

By Naoman Saeed, Founder of Trogdyne

How to use this checklist: Don't guess. Test. Go through each of the 15 items below and perform the exact "How to check" action. Be brutally honest. Check the box if you pass. The counter below will update in real-time to show your score.

Your Score: 0 / 15

Section 1: First Impressions & Visual Trust

How to check: Open your homepage. Read only the largest text (the H1 heading). Does it state exactly what you do and who you serve in under 10 words, without requiring the user to scroll?

How to check: Open your site on your actual smartphone. Is your primary "Book Now" or "Contact" button visible without scrolling, and can you tap it easily with your thumb?

How to check: Paste your homepage URL into Google PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev). Is your "Mobile" performance score 80 or higher?

How to check: Count the items in your main desktop navigation menu. Are there 5 or fewer items (e.g., Home, Services, About, Projects, Contact)?

How to check: Look at the photos on your homepage. Are they authentic photos of your actual business, team, or work, rather than generic, recognizable stock photography?

Section 2: Content & Communication

How to check: Starting from your homepage, count the clicks required to find your email address or a contact form. Is it 2 clicks or fewer?

How to check: Scroll through your homepage. Do you have at least 2 verifiable client testimonials, case study snippets, or recognizable client logos displayed prominently?

How to check: Is there a photo of you or your team, along with a brief, personal "About" statement, visible directly on the homepage?

How to check: Review your entire homepage. Are you using no more than 2 distinct font families (e.g., one for headings, one for body text)?

How to check: Look at your website's URL in the browser bar. Does it start with https:// and display a padlock icon?

Section 3: Conversion & User Experience

How to check: Look at the bottom of your homepage. Is there exactly ONE dominant, visually distinct call-to-action button telling the user exactly what to do next?

How to check: Open your contact form. Does it take fewer than 60 seconds to fill out, requiring no more than Name, Email, and a Message?

How to check: Click every single link in your main navigation, footer, and body text. Do they all load the correct page instantly without a "404 Not Found" error?

How to check: Does your site feature custom brand colors and intentional spacing that clearly deviate from the default, out-of-the-box settings of Wix, Squarespace, or standard themes?

How to check: Scroll to your website footer. Is there a clearly visible, clickable link to your Privacy Policy?

How Did You Score?

  • 13–15 "Yes" Answers: Excellent. Your foundation is solid. Focus on scaling your traffic.
  • 8–12 "Yes" Answers: Good, but you have hidden friction points that are silently turning away potential clients.
  • 7 or Fewer "Yes" Answers: Critical. Your website is likely damaging your brand credibility and leaking revenue.
