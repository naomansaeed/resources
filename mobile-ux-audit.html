Section 1: Touch Target Optimization 3 questions

How to check: Use your browser's developer tools (right-click → Inspect) to measure button dimensions. Or use a ruler on your phone screen—buttons should be at least 7mm wide.
Note: Small buttons cause "fat-finger" errors and frustrate mobile users. We can audit your touch targets and resize them to meet mobile UX standards.
How to check: Look at your mobile site. Can you tap one button without accidentally hitting another? Use developer tools to measure the gap between elements.
Note: Inadequate spacing causes mis-taps and user frustration. We can add proper spacing between your interactive elements.
How to check: Hold your phone in one hand. Can you tap your main CTA with your thumb without stretching? It should be in the bottom 50% of the screen.
Note: The "thumb zone" is the area easily reachable with one hand. Placing your CTA here can significantly increase mobile conversions. We can restructure your mobile layout to optimize for thumb reach.

Section 2: Mobile Readability 2 questions

How to check: Open your site on your phone. Can you read the main text without zooming in? If you have to pinch-to-zoom, your text is too small.
Note: Small text forces users to zoom, creating friction. We can ensure your typography is mobile-optimized and readable without zooming.
How to check: Use a contrast checker tool (like WebAIM's) to test your text colors. Light gray text on white backgrounds often fails this test.
Note: Poor contrast makes text hard to read, especially in bright sunlight. We can adjust your color palette to meet accessibility standards.

Section 3: Mobile Form UX 2 questions

How to check: Tap on your email field. Does the keyboard show "@" and ".com"? Tap on a phone field. Does it show the number pad? If not, you're using the wrong input types.
Note: Using proper input types triggers the correct keyboard, reducing typing errors. We can update your form fields to use the right input types.
How to check: Submit your form with invalid data. Do you see clear, specific error messages like "Please enter a valid email address" or generic messages like "Error"?
Note: Vague error messages frustrate users and cause form abandonment. We can implement clear, specific error messaging.

Section 4: Mobile Navigation 2 questions

How to check: Scroll down your mobile site. Does your main CTA stay visible (sticky/fixed), or does it disappear as you scroll?
Note: A sticky CTA follows the user as they scroll, making it easy to book at any time. We can implement a persistent CTA that stays visible on mobile.
How to check: Look at your mobile site. Is there a clearly visible hamburger menu (☰) or menu button? Can you open it with one tap?
Note: Hidden or hard-to-find menus frustrate mobile users. We can ensure your navigation is easily accessible.

Section 5: Technical Mobile Issues 1 question

How to check: Swipe left and right on your mobile site. Does the page move horizontally? If yes, you have elements that are too wide for mobile screens.
Note: Horizontal scrolling is a major mobile UX failure. We can fix layout issues causing horizontal scroll.
