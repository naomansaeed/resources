Custom Branding 4 questions

How to check: Look at your logo. Was it created by a designer specifically for your brand, or did you type your business name into a Canva/Wix template and save it?
How to check: Hold your business card (or open your Instagram profile) next to your website. Do the colors feel like they belong to the same brand, or do they look unrelated?
How to check: Open your website in a browser tab. Do you see a custom icon (your logo or a brand symbol), or just a generic globe, letter, or blank square?
How to check: Look at your contact page or footer. Does the email address use your own domain name, or is it a free email service like Gmail, Yahoo, or Hotmail?

Visual Quality 3 questions

How to check: Scroll through your homepage. Do the gaps between sections feel uniform, or do some sections feel squeezed together while others have too much empty space?
How to check: Look at all the images on your homepage and key pages. Do they feel like they belong together, or is it a jarring mix of crisp photos, blurry ones, and different aspect ratios?
How to check: Look at any page on your site. Can you instantly tell which text is a heading and which is body copy, or do they blur together at similar sizes?

Trust Signals 4 questions

How to check: Read your testimonials. Do they include the client's real first and last name, and do they mention specific results or experiences (e.g., "Jane Smith said our redesign increased her bookings by 40%")?
How to check: Look for a photo of yourself or your team. Is it a clear, well-lit professional headshot or team photo, or a casual snapshot that wouldn't look out of place on personal social media?
How to check: Look for a section showing logos of clients you've worked with, badges of certifications you hold, awards you've won, or professional organizations you belong to. If you have none of these, answer "No."
How to check: Look for a dedicated section on your site that showcases your past projects, client work, or case studies with before/after results. If you don't have this section, answer "No."

Content Polish 2 questions

How to check: Read through the text on your homepage, about page, and services page. Are there any typos, grammatical mistakes, or awkward phrasing that a professional editor would catch?
How to check: Read your homepage copy. Count how many times it says "you/your" versus "we/I/me/my." If "you" appears more often, answer "Yes."

Design Sophistication 1 question

How to check: Count the distinct colors used across your entire website (backgrounds, text, buttons, accents). If you see more than 4 clearly different colors, answer "No."
